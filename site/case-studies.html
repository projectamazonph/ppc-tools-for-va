1. Home & Lifestyle Brand — From 45% to 18% ACoS

ACoS
$2.1K → $8.5K

$500 → $1.5K

The Challenge

$25 price point, 40% margin, but ACoS was killing profitability. Running 15 auto campaigns with no manual campaigns. Zero search term optimization.

What We Did

  • Created proper campaign structure: 1 auto campaign for discovery + 3 manual exact match campaigns for top performers
  • Implemented weekly search term harvesting — moved converting terms to manual campaigns
  • Added 200+ negative keywords to filter irrelevant traffic
  • Set up bid rules: reduce bids by 15% when ACoS exceeds 25%

The Challenge

Account in chaos: 200 campaigns, most with $1/day budget, 90% ACoS, client was ready to quit. $50 product, 45% margin.

What We Did

  • Week 1: Audited all campaigns, identified 15 with data, killed 185
  • Restructured to 12 campaigns: 3 auto (discovery), 9 manual (targeted)
  • Implemented strict negative keyword strategy
  • Set minimum $15/day budget per campaign for meaningful data
